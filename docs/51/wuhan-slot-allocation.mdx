# Slot Allocation Plan for the 51st ICPC Asia Regional Contest (Wuhan)

> [Original Chinese version](https://icpc.pku.edu.cn/tzgg/35fbb9f94ae841dfbd64bcd332ef4f86.htm)

The **51st ICPC Asia Regional Contest (Wuhan)** is scheduled to be held on **October 31 – November 1, 2026**. Based on venue capacity, the onsite contest is expected to offer approximately **400–450 seats**, with **no more than 400 official participation slots**. In accordance with ICPC rules and the relevant requirements of the Asia Regional Contests, the Organizing Committee has established the following slot allocation policy for the onsite contest:

## Slot Allocation Rules

1. **Online Qualifier ranking slots**  
   - Universities ranked **1st through 80th** in the 51st ICPC Asia EC Online Qualifier → **2 official slots** each.  
   - Universities ranked **81st through 180th** → **1 official slot** each.  
   This rule allocates approximately **260 official slots** in total.

2. **Hosting and problem‑setting bonus**  
   For the 51st ICPC Asia EC Online Qualifier, Regional Contests, and the EC Final, each university that serves as a **host or problem‑setter** receives **2 official slots** (if multiple universities co‑host a site, the 2 slots are shared equally among them).  
   This rule allocates approximately **25 official slots**.

3. **World Finals participation bonus**  
   Each Chinese university that qualified for any of the **48th, 49th, or 50th ICPC World Finals** receives **1 official slot**.  
   This rule allocates approximately **30 official slots**.

4. **2026 ICPC National Invitational Contest (Wuhan) slots**  
   Each of the top **60** universities in the ranking of the 2026 ICPC National Invitational Contest (Wuhan) receives **1 onsite slot**.  
   This rule allocates approximately **60 official slots**.

5. **Provincial contribution slots**  
   Universities that have contributed to the popularisation and development of programming contests in **Hubei Province** or that have provided assistance to the programming contest activities at **Wuhan University** may apply for remaining official slots after registration opens.

6. **Unofficial ("starred") slots**  
   Any remaining seats at this site will be made available as starred (unofficial) slots.

## Overall Limit

The above slots are **cumulative** for each university. However:

- Universities that have advanced to the **48th, 49th, or 50th World Finals**, or that are **hosts or problem‑setters** for the 51st Asia EC Regionals, are capped at **4 official teams**.
- All other universities are capped at **3 official teams**.

## Final Provisions

Any matters not explicitly covered herein shall be interpreted by the Organizing Committee.
