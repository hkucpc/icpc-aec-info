# Slot Allocation Plan for the 51st ICPC Asia EC Regional Contest (Shenyang)

> [Original Chinese version](https://icpc.pku.edu.cn/tzgg/2b4f6bc1a27d4b4c91b6b5a4c78f97f3.htm)

The **51st ICPC Asia EC Regional Contest (Shenyang)** is scheduled to be held on **November 14–15, 2026** at the **Northeastern University (Nanhu Campus)**. In accordance with ICPC EC rules and the relevant requirements of the Asia Regional Committee, the Organizing Committee has established the following slot allocation policy for the onsite contest.

## I. Total Onsite Slots

Based on the venue capacity, the Shenyang site will offer **400 participation slots**. The Organizing Committee reserves the right to adjust the total number of teams according to actual registration conditions.

## II. Detailed Allocation Scheme

- Host universities and problem‑setting universities of the 51st ICPC Asia EC Regional Contest may send **no more than 4 teams** to the onsite contest.
- General universities may send **no more than 3 teams** to the onsite contest.

### 1. Online Qualifier Slots – 320 slots

A total of **320 slots** are allocated from the Online Qualifier based on the final ranking:

- a) Universities ranked **1st through 100th** → **2 slots** each → **200 slots** total.
- b) Universities ranked **101st through 220th** → **1 slot** each → **120 slots** total.

### 2. Contribution Bonus Slots – 50 slots

A total of **50 slots** are allocated as contribution bonuses, including:

- a) **World Finals participation bonus** – Each university that participated in any of the **last three ICPC World Finals (48th, 49th, or 50th)** receives **1 bonus slot**.
- b) **Hosting bonus** – Each host university of the **51st ICPC Asia EC Regional Contest** receives **2 slots**.
- c) **Problem‑setting bonus** – Each university that contributed problems to the **51st ICPC Asia EC Regional Contest** receives **2 slots** (Note: per contest site, the total problem‑setting bonus for each such university is 2 slots in total).

### 3. Women’s Team Slots – 10 slots

A total of **10 slots** are reserved for **women’s teams** (i.e., teams where **all members are female**) to encourage and support greater participation of female contestants.

- Universities may apply for **one women’s team slot**.
- Allocation of women’s team slots is based on the **school ranking in the Online Qualifier**.
- **Women’s team slots do not count toward** the per‑university team limit (i.e., they are exempt from the general caps of 4 for host/problem‑setting universities and 3 for general universities).

### 4. Provincial Contribution Slots – 20 slots

A total of **20 slots** are allocated to universities that have contributed to the popularisation and development of programming contests in **Liaoning Province**, or that have provided assistance to the programming contest activities at **Northeastern University**.

- Eligible universities may apply for **1 onsite slot**.
- Any unused slots after this allocation will be opened for application by other universities in need, with the application method to be announced in the registration guide.

### 5. Unofficial ("Starred") Teams

Where venue capacity permits, universities may apply to participate as **starred (unofficial) teams**. Applications are subject to review and approval by the Organizing Committee. The application method for starred teams will be announced in the registration guide.

## III. Additional Provisions

The interpretation of any matters not explicitly covered in this slot allocation plan rests with the **Shenyang Site Organizing Committee**.
