# Slot Allocation Plan for the 51st ICPC Asia Regional Contest (Nanchang)

> [Original Chinese version](https://icpc.pku.edu.cn/tzgg/b4d55e13589b47d384b6c854702acc86.htm)

The **51st ICPC Asia Regional Contest (Nanchang)** is scheduled to be held on **December 19–20, 2026**. The slot allocation policy for the onsite contest is as follows:

## I. Total Onsite Slots

The expected total number of slots available is approximately **360**.

## II. Sources of Slot Allocation

Slots for the Nanchang onsite contest are derived from the following sources:

1. **Qualification slots** recommended from the **Online Qualifier**;
2. **Qualification slots** recommended from the **National Invitational Contest (Nanchang)**;
3. **EC Contribution Bonus Slots**;
4. **Support and incentive slots**.

## III. Detailed Slot Distribution

### 1. Online Qualifier – approximately 220 slots

Based on the final ranking of the **2026 ICPC Asia EC Online Qualifier**:
- Universities ranked **1st through 60th** → **2 slots** each;
- Universities ranked **61st through 160th** → **1 slot** each.

Total slots from the Online Qualifier: approximately **220**.

### 2. National Invitational Contest (Nanchang) – approximately 100 slots

Based on the results of the **2026 ICPC National Invitational Contest (Nanchang)** (held on May 17, 2026):
- Each university that wins a **silver medal or higher** (i.e., gold or silver) receives **1 onsite slot**.

Total slots from the National Invitational Contest: approximately **100**.

### 3. EC Contribution Bonus Slots

These consist of two parts:

- **(1) World Finals participation bonus** – Each university that advanced to any of the **last three ICPC World Finals (48th, 49th, or 50th)** receives **1 slot**.  
  This amounts to approximately **31 slots**.

- **(2) Hosting & problem‑setting bonus** – Each host site of the **2026 Asia EC Regional Contests** and each institution that contributed problems (including the Online Qualifier) receives **2 slots**.  
  This yields approximately **26 slots**.

### 4. Support and Incentive Slots

Any slots remaining after the allocations from rules 1–3 above will be opened for application directly to the Organizing Committee.  
Preference will be given to:
- Universities that have provided help to the EC region or to our university’s development, and
- Non‑mainland‑China universities within the EC region.

## IV. Additional Notes

1. **Per‑university maximum for Nanchang** –  
   - Universities that have previously advanced to the World Finals, or are hosts/problem‑setters for the 2026 Asia EC Regionals (including the Online Qualifier), are generally capped at **4 slots**.  
   - All other universities are capped at **3 slots**.

2. **Drop‑down (fallback) rule** –  
   If any slots remain unused after the above distributions, they will be offered **first to universities that have not yet received any slot**, based on the rankings of the Online Qualifier and the National Invitational Contest.
