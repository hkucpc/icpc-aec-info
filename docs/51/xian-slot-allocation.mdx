# Slot Allocation Plan for the 51st ICPC Asia Regional Contest (Xi’an)

> [Original Chinese version](https://icpc.pku.edu.cn/tzgg/a9259fcfb12d4087b31bc1eef3490e79.htm)

The **51st ICPC Asia Regional Contest (Xi’an)** is scheduled to be held on **October 17–18, 2026**. In accordance with ICPC rules and the requirements of the Asia Regional Contests, the Organizing Committee has established the following slot allocation policy for the onsite contest.

## I. Total Onsite Slots

The Xi’an regional onsite contest will offer a total of **380 slots**.

## II. Sources of Slot Allocation

Slots for the Xi’an onsite contest are derived from the following sources:

1. **Qualification slots** recommended from the **2026 ICPC Asia EC Online Qualifier**;
2. **Qualification slots** recommended from the **2026 ICPC National Invitational Contest (Shaanxi)**;
3. **Bonus and contribution slots** recommended by the **ICPC Asia EC Committee**;
4. **Qualification slots** recommended by **provincial‑level exclusive contests** as determined by ICPC Asia;
5. **Support and incentive slots** based on the principle of **regional balance**.

## III. Detailed Slot Distribution

### 1. Online Qualifier – 230 slots

Based on the final ranking of the **2026 ICPC Asia EC Online Qualifier**:
- Universities ranked **1st through 80th** → **2 slots** each;
- Universities ranked **81st through 150th** → **1 slot** each.

Total slots from the Online Qualifier: **230**.

### 2. National Invitational Contest (Shaanxi) – 100 slots

Based on the results of the **2026 ICPC National Invitational Contest (Shaanxi)** (held on May 2, 2026):
- The top **100 universities** in the ranking → **1 slot** each for the Xi’an onsite contest.

Total slots from the National Invitational Contest: **100**.

### 3. EC Contribution Bonus Slots – approximately 55 slots

These consist of two parts:

- **(1) World Finals participation bonus** – Each university that advanced to the **2025 or 2026 ICPC World Finals** receives **1 slot**.  
  This amounts to approximately **25 slots** (one per qualifying university).

- **(2) Hosting & problem‑setting bonus** – Each host site of the **2026 Asia EC Regional Contests** and each institution that contributed problems (including the Online Qualifier) receives **2 slots**.  
  With about 15 such institutions, this yields approximately **30 slots**.

Total contribution bonus slots ≈ **25 + 30 = 55**.

- **2025 World Finals**:  
  Peking University, Tsinghua University, Zhejiang University, University of Science and Technology of China, Sun Yat‑sen University, Beihang University, Harbin Institute of Technology, Shanghai Jiao Tong University, Central South University, Beijing Jiaotong University, Nanjing University of Science and Technology, Southern University of Science and Technology, Beijing University of Posts and Telecommunications, Nanjing University of Aeronautics and Astronautics, Shanghai University, The University of Hong Kong

- **2026 World Finals**:  
  Peking University, Tsinghua University, Zhejiang University, Shanghai Jiao Tong University, Fudan University, Sun Yat‑sen University, Wuhan University, The Chinese University of Hong Kong, University of Science and Technology of China, Harbin Institute of Technology, University of Electronic Science and Technology of China, Jilin University, Northeastern University, Guangdong University of Technology, Northwestern Polytechnical University, The Chinese University of Hong Kong (Shenzhen)

**List of 2026 Asia EC Regional host institutions** (eligible for hosting bonus):  
Peking University, Northwestern Polytechnical University, Northeastern University, University of Electronic Science and Technology of China, Wuhan University, Nanjing University of Aeronautics and Astronautics, Jiangxi Normal University, Shanghai University, The University of Hong Kong, Hangzhou Normal University, Zhejiang University

### 4. Remaining Slots (Sources 4 and 5)

After the allocations from rules 1–3, any remaining slots will be reserved for **Sources 4 and 5** (provincial‑level exclusive contests and regional balance incentives). Interested universities should contact the Organizing Committee directly for recommendation or application via their respective provincial contests or regional bodies.

### 5. Additional Limitations (To Broaden Coverage)

- **(1) Per‑university maximum for Xi’an** –  
  - Universities that have previously advanced to the World Finals, or are hosts/problem‑setters for the 2026 Asia EC Regionals (including the Online Qualifier), are generally capped at **4 slots** for Xi’an.  
  - All other universities are capped at **3 slots**.

- **(2) Combined slots from invitations, provincial contests, and EC support** –  
  Each university may receive **at most 2 slots in total** from the National Invitational Contest, exclusive provincial contests, or EC support slots combined.

### 6. Drop‑Down (Fallback) Rule

If any slots remain unused after the above distributions, they will be offered **first to universities that have not yet received any slot**, based on the rankings of the Online Qualifier and the National Invitational Contest. Where venue capacity permits, such universities may also apply to participate as **starred (unofficial) teams**.
