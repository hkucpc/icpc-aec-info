# Slot Allocation Plan for the 51st ICPC Asia Regional Contest (Chengdu)

> [Original Chinese version](https://icpc.pku.edu.cn/tzgg/19c9af66a6184e29a863547a2f83cc74.htm)

Authorised by the **ICPC Asia Regional Committee**, the 51st ICPC Asia Regional Contest (Chengdu) is scheduled to be held **offline** on **October 24–25, 2026** at the **Pidu District Sports Center Gymnasium**. The total number of onsite teams is expected to be approximately **320**. In accordance with ICPC rules and the relevant requirements of the ICPC Asia Regional Committee, the following slot allocation plan for the Chengdu onsite contest has been established:

## Slot Allocation Rules

1. **Online Qualifier ranking slots**  
   Each of the top **160** universities in the final ranking of the **2026 ICPC Asia EC Online Qualifier** will receive **1 onsite slot**.  
   → Total allocated: **160 slots**.

2. **Multiple top‑500 teams in the Online Qualifier**  
   If a university has **3 or more teams** ranked among the top 500 in the same Online Qualifier, that university will receive **1 additional onsite slot**.  
   This is expected to allocate approximately **80 slots**.  
   If the number of qualifying universities exceeds the venue capacity, allocation will be determined based on the number of top‑500 teams each university has and their respective rankings.

3. **World Finals participation bonus**  
   Each Chinese university that qualified for the **48th, 49th, or 50th ICPC World Finals** will receive **1 onsite slot**.

4. **Hosting and problem‑setting bonus**  
   - Each university that **hosts** a 51st ICPC Asia Regional Contest site receives **2 additional slots**.  
   - Each university that **contributes problems** to a 51st ICPC Asia Regional Contest (including the Online Qualifier) receives **2 additional slots**.

5. **Contribution to Sichuan provincial programming contest development**  
   Universities that have made significant contributions to the popularisation and development of programming contests in **Sichuan Province** may apply for **1 slot**.

## Overall Limit and Reallocation

- The above slots are **cumulative** for each university.  
- However, the **total number of onsite teams** from any single university for the Chengdu regional shall **not exceed 4**.

- Any unused slots after the above allocations will be **opened for application** by universities in need.

## Application Method

The application procedure for Chengdu onsite slots will be announced **after the conclusion of the 2026 ICPC Asia EC Online Qualifier**.
