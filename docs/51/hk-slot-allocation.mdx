# Slot Allocation Plan for the 51st ICPC Asia Regional Contest (Hong Kong & Macau)

> [Original Chinese version](https://icpc.pku.edu.cn/tzgg/374c9f5210bd4ff287068ec82d56dde2.htm)

The **51st ICPC Asia Regional Contest (Hong Kong & Macau)** is scheduled to be held as an **onsite contest** on **January 9–10, 2027**. The contest will be hosted by **The University of Hong Kong**.

The onsite contest can accommodate approximately **120 teams** in total, with about **5 slots reserved for unofficial ("starred") teams** (the exact number depends on the final registration count of official teams).

## 1. Allocation for Universities outside Hong Kong and Macau

A total of **95 slots** are allocated for official participation to universities **outside** Hong Kong and Macau. The allocation proceeds in three rounds:

- **(1) First round** — By **October 30**, each university may register **one team** freely.  
  If the number of registered teams exceeds 100, selection will be based on the **Online Qualifier ranking**, with each university receiving **at most one slot** in this round.

- **(2) Second round** — By **November 14**, any unused slots from the first round will be redistributed.  
  Each university may register **one additional team** on a **first‑come, first‑served** basis.

- **(3) Third round** — Any slots still unused after the second round will be open for application by interested universities. The host will review all applications and allocate the remaining slots centrally.

## 2. Allocation for Universities in Hong Kong and Macau

A total of **20 slots** are allocated for official participation to universities **in** Hong Kong and Macau.  
Each university (including the host, The University of Hong Kong) may send **up to 2 teams**.

Applications must be submitted by the requesting universities and will be reviewed and centrally allocated by the host.

## 3. Reallocation of Remaining Slots

After the above allocation rounds, if any slots remain unused due to insufficient registration or withdrawals, **The University of Hong Kong** (the host) will take into account the contributions of all units to the preparation of the regional contest and allocate the remaining slots at its discretion.

## 4. Additional Notes

- **(1) Starred teams** include:
  - Teams from universities that exceed the slot limits, and
  - A small number of secondary school teams from Hong Kong and Macau.  
  **Starred teams do not participate in the official rankings.**

- **(2) The final interpretation** of this plan and any matters not explicitly covered herein rests with the **Organizing Committee of the 51st ICPC Asia Regional Contest (Hong Kong & Macau)**.
