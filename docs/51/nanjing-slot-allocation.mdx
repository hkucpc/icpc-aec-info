# Slot Allocation Plan for the 51st ICPC Asia Regional Contest (Nanjing)

> [Original Chinese version](https://icpc.pku.edu.cn/tzgg/276a166ac0c34db0af139941fbf2323c.htm)

The **51st ICPC Asia Regional Contest (Nanjing)** is scheduled to be held as an onsite contest on **November 7–8, 2026** at the **Jiangjun Road Campus of Nanjing University of Aeronautics and Astronautics**. The estimated total capacity is approximately **320 teams**. In accordance with ICPC rules and the relevant requirements of the ICPC Asia EC Regional Committee, the following slot allocation plan for onsite participation has been established:

## Slot Allocation Rules

1. **Online Qualifier ranking slots**  
   Each of the top **160** universities in the final ranking of the **2026 ICPC Asia EC Online Qualifier** will receive **1 official team slot**.  
   → Total: **160 slots**.

2. **Multiple top‑500 teams in the Online Qualifier**  
   If a university has **3 or more teams** ranked among the top 500 in the same Online Qualifier, that university will receive **1 additional official team slot**.  
   This is expected to allocate approximately **76 slots**.  
   If the number of qualifying universities exceeds the venue capacity, allocation will be determined based on the number of top‑500 teams each university has and their respective rankings.

3. **World Finals participation bonus**  
   Each Chinese university that qualified for the **48th, 49th, or 50th ICPC World Finals** will receive **1 official team slot**.  
   → Total: approximately **30 slots**.

4. **Hosting and problem‑setting bonus**  
   - Host universities of the **50th or 51st ICPC Asia EC Regional Contests** (including the Online Qualifier) receive **2 official team slots**.  
   - Universities in the EC region that contributed problems to the **51st ICPC Asia EC Regional Contest** receive **2 official team slots**.  
   → Expected total: approximately **24 slots**.

5. **Wildcard for non‑mainland‑China EC universities**  
   Each university from the ICPC Asia EC region **outside Mainland China** may apply for **1 official team slot**.  
   → Expected total: approximately **10 slots**.

6. **Contribution to Jiangsu Provincial Programming Contest development**  
   Universities that have contributed to the popularisation and development of programming contests in **Jiangsu Province** (e.g., by hosting the Jiangsu Provincial Programming Contest) or that have provided assistance to the programming contest activities at **Nanjing University of Aeronautics and Astronautics** may apply for **1 official team slot**.  
   → Expected total: approximately **20 slots**.

## Overall Limit

The above slots are **cumulative** for each university. However, the **total number of official teams** from any single university for the Nanjing regional shall **not exceed 4**.

## Reallocation of Unused Slots

Any unused slots after the above allocations will be opened for application by universities in need. The application method will be announced **after the conclusion of the 2026 ICPC Asia EC Online Qualifier**.
