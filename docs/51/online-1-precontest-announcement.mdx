# Pre‑Contest Announcement

## 2026 ICPC Asia EC Online Qualifier 1

For this contest, we would like to remind all participants of the following important points to ensure a smooth competition:

1. Please read the [Contestant Handbook](./online-handbook) carefully and complete the OMS proctoring system self‑test as required. **Be sure to test the latest version of OMS in advance** – issues discovered right before the contest may not receive a timely response.

2. Contest time: **September 6, 13:00 – 18:00**. The system will allow login starting **1 hour before** the contest. Once logged in, **do not touch your mouse or keyboard** until the contest begins.

3. **The exam number and name required for login will be distributed by each school’s registration administrator via the registration website. Please use your registered account to access the following link to retrieve your ticket: [^1].**  
   This contest does **not** require pre‑approval for login; participants are **not required** to submit environment videos or selfie photos. Identity verification will take place **after the contest starts**. Accordingly, participants **must not leave early** before their identity has been checked, otherwise their results may be invalidated.

4. Any participant who has **not logged in by 13:15** on September 6 will be **unable to log in**.

5. After login and during the contest, the proctor will pop up windows one by one requesting participants to show their **student ID** or other credentials. Please keep an eye on these notifications from the proctor. If necessary, the proctor may make a voice call via the mini‑program – **set your phone volume to maximum** and respond promptly when called to avoid unnecessary complications.

6. Adjust your camera setup **before the contest starts** to avoid wasting time during the contest due to non‑compliant positioning.

7. **Clear the project/file list of your competition machine** before the official contest begins. Do **not** open any existing code to avoid unnecessary problems.

8. Maintain a distance of **at least 2 meters** between competing teams.

9. **Wireless keyboards and mice are not allowed.** Bluetooth must be turned off.

10. At the start of the contest, the **team leader’s machine** will begin first; the other team members’ machines will gain contest access about **5 minutes later**. A notification regarding the start for team members will be displayed in the proctoring client.

11. **Exam Code (QR Code):** [^2]

12. Prohibited Actions

    **Violation of any of the following will be treated as cheating:**

    1. Leaving the exam area within the first **15 minutes** after the contest starts.

    2. Using virtual machines.

    3. Using dual monitors, regardless of whether the second screen is turned on.

    4. Wearing earphones, smartwatches, or other electronic devices.

    5. Touching the USB ports during the contest.

    6. Accessing **any** website in any form.

    7. Using **any form of artificial intelligence (AI) assistance** tools.

    8. Opening any electronic materials stored on the machine beforehand.

    9. Inserting obfuscated code into your submissions.

    10. Copying or screenshoting problem statements to other files.

    11. Using any electronic translation tools.

    12. Communicating with anyone other than the proctor.

    13. Refusing to comply with the proctor’s inspection requests.

    14. Turning off the camera or the proctoring client without permission during inspections.

    15. Using **any form of instant messaging tools**.  
        Using **any assistive features that exceed beginner‑level programming ability** – including but not limited to large language models (LLMs), non‑dictionary smart code completion tools, program analysis tools, etc.

Wishing you all great results!
