# Slot Allocation Plan for the 51st ICPC Asia Regional Contest (Shanghai)

> [Original Chinese version](https://icpc.pku.edu.cn/tzgg/7d3cbb4896de4774873be489d780a82b.htm)

The **51st ICPC Asia Regional Contest (Shanghai)** is scheduled to be held as an **onsite contest** on **December 5–6, 2026** at the **Baoshan Campus of Shanghai University**. The venue can accommodate approximately **384 teams** in total, including about **336 official teams** and about **48 unofficial ("starred") teams**.

## I. Official Team Slot Allocation

### 1. Slots from the Online Qualifier – 250 official slots

- (1) Based on the final ranking of the **51st ICPC Asia Online Qualifier**, the top 50 universities (ranks 1–50) will each receive **2 slots** – a total of **100 slots**.

- (2) Universities ranked 51st through 200th in the Online Qualifier will each receive **1 slot** – a total of **150 slots**.

### 2. Bonus slots and wildcard slots – 86 official slots

These are allocated as follows:

- (1) **World Finals participation bonus** – Each university that participated in any of the **last three ICPC World Finals (48th, 49th, or 50th)** receives **1 bonus slot**.

- (2) **Hosting and problem‑setting bonus** – Apart from the host institution itself, every university that:
  - hosted the **50th or 51st ICPC Asia Regional Contest**, or
  - contributed problems to the **51st ICPC Asia Regional Contest**  
  shall receive **2 bonus slots** per university (if a university qualifies under multiple criteria, it still receives only 2 slots in total).

- (3) **Wildcard for non‑mainland‑China universities** – Each university from the **ICPC Asia East Division outside Mainland China** may apply for **1 wildcard slot**.

- (4) **Wildcard for supporting the 2026 Shanghai Collegiate Programming Contest** – Each university that provided assistance to that contest may apply for **1 wildcard slot**.

- (5) **Reallocation of unused bonus slots** – Any slots not used from the above categories will be converted into additional wildcard slots. These wildcards are subject to application by interested universities and final approval by the Organizing Committee.

## II. Official Slot Allocation Procedure

After the **first‑round** registration closes, any unused slots will be made available as **wildcards** in a **second‑round allocation**. In the second round, interested universities must submit applications, which will be reviewed and approved by the Organizing Committee.

## III. Slot Limit per University (First Round)

In the first round of allocation, the general limit is:

- **At most 3 official teams** per university.

However, the following universities are allowed **up to 4 official teams**:
- Host universities of the **51st ICPC Asia Regional Contest**,
- Universities that contributed problems to the **51st ICPC Asia Regional Contest**, and
- Universities located in **Shanghai**.

## IV. Unofficial ("Starred") Teams

Starred teams **do not participate in the official rankings**. They include:
- Teams from **Shanghai University**,
- Teams composed of **enterprise or non‑student personnel**,
- **Extra teams** from any university that exceed the official slot limit, and
- A small number of **primary or secondary school teams**.

All starred team entries must be applied for by the respective organisations and are subject to approval by the Organizing Committee.

## V. Final Provisions

The interpretation of this plan, as well as any matters not explicitly covered herein, rests with the **Organizing Committee of the 51st ICPC Asia Regional Contest (Shanghai)**.
