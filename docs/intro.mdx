# ICPC Asia EC Info

**ICPC Asia EC Info** is a **non-official, community-driven** project. Its goal is to make official ICPC Asia EC documents more accessible to international contestants, coaches, and volunteers who are more comfortable reading in English.

- **Not affiliated with ICPC Asia EC or ICPC Foundation.**
- All translations are produced by volunteers and may contain errors.
- When in doubt, the **official Chinese versions** always take precedence.
